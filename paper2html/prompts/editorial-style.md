# WeChat Editorial Style

- Write in Chinese for scientifically literate non-specialists.
- Lead with the scientific problem and the paper's answer, not publication prestige.
- Define each specialist term in plain language at first mention.
- Separate what the authors demonstrated, what they inferred, and what remains uncertain.
- Preserve dataset names, sample sizes, metrics, baselines, and experimental conditions when they qualify a claim.
- Avoid hype such as "颠覆性" or "彻底解决" unless the paper directly supports it.
- Use short paragraphs and informative headings. Do not use emoji or marketing calls to action.
- End with a balanced summary that states both contribution and boundary.
